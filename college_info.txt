2. ABOUT THE COLLEGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Kings College of Engineering (KCE) was founded in 2001 by Raj Educational Trust
(RET), Chennai, with the aim of providing quality technical education to the
economically disadvantaged and rural community. KCE is approved by the All
India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi, and affiliated to
Anna University, Chennai. It is a NAAC-accredited institution and has been
conferred AUTONOMOUS STATUS by UGC, New Delhi, and Anna University, Chennai.
It is also ISO certified (TUV, Rheinland, Germany) and NBA accredited (select
programmes).

The institution offers 7 UG and 5 PG programmes (including a newly introduced
MBA), plus Ph.D. programmes. The Departments of Electronics & Communication
Engineering and Mechanical Engineering have Research Centres recognized by
Anna University, Chennai (Civil and ECE are also noted as AU-recognized
research centres in some records).

Motto: "Seek, Strive, Succeed"
Around 45-50% of students belong to the First Graduate (FG) category.

6. TNEA & ADMISSION PROCESS (UG - B.E./B.Tech)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(Compiled from third-party admission portals; verify current-year specifics
on tnea.ac.in and the college's own admission notices.)

- Admission to UG B.E./B.Tech seats is through TNEA (Tamil Nadu Engineering
  Admissions) counselling, conducted by Anna University, Chennai.
- Eligibility: Passed 10+2 with Science stream subjects, minimum 45% marks
  (40% for reserved category candidates) OR a Diploma in Engineering/
  Technology with minimum 45% marks (40% for reserved category).
- General Admission Process:
    1. Register on the TNEA portal during the counselling window (dates
       announced annually by Anna University/DoTE - typically opens
       May/June).
    2. Fill in preferences (college & branch choices).
    3. Upload required documents (mark sheets, community/income certificates,
       photograph, etc.) as per TNEA requirements.
    4. Attend counselling (online random/rank-based allotment) and confirm
       seat allotment at KCE if allotted.
    5. Report to the college with original documents for admission
       confirmation and fee payment.
  (Some categories - Management Quota / NRI, if applicable - may follow a
  separate direct-application process through the college's own admission
  cell; contact the college's Information Centre at Thanjavur or
  contact@kingsengg.edu.in for current-year specifics.)
- PG (M.E./M.B.A) admissions: Through TANCET counselling / Anna University
  norms.
- TNEA Cut-off Ranks (2024, indicative, General category - Home State rank):
    B.Tech AI & Data Science          : Opening ~28,326 - Closing ~64,261
    B.Tech Information Technology     : ~69,960 (both open/close)
    B.E. Civil Engineering            : ~135,530 - ~191,486
    B.E. Computer Science & Engg.     : ~34,155 - ~86,861
    B.E. Electrical & Electronics Eng.: ~95,522 (both open/close)
    B.E. Electronics & Communication  : ~27,675 - ~97,845
    B.E. Mechanical Engineering       : ~112,938 (both open/close)
  (Cut-offs vary every year and by category - General/OBC/SC/ST/MBC, etc.
  Always check the latest official TNEA cut-off list for the current year.)
